# Getting help

Start with the [README](README.md), [cloud setup](docs/operations/cloud-deployment.md), [Apple setup](docs/operations/apple-testflight.md), and [recovery guide](docs/operations/recovery.md).

For setup questions, search the [issues](https://github.com/artemf375/relay/issues). If there is no answer, open a [help request](https://github.com/artemf375/relay/issues/new?template=help.yml). Include your Relay commit or version, deployment type, relevant tool versions, what you tried, and a redacted error message.

Use the [bug form](https://github.com/artemf375/relay/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) for a reproducible defect and the [feature form](https://github.com/artemf375/relay/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) for a proposed change. Report suspected vulnerabilities privately through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Issues are public. Remove tokens, private keys, hostnames you want to keep private, device identifiers, and notification content from logs and screenshots. Never upload a database or your CLI configuration.

Relay is maintained on a best-effort basis. Support and response times are not guaranteed. Hosting accounts, billing, and Apple signing access are managed by their respective providers. A successful APNs response does not prove that the phone displayed a notification.
